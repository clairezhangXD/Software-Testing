What are some competing quality attributes?
- security vs usability vs accessibility
having more security checks in place can cause a complex user experience

- flexibility vs simplicity 
having a more flexible design or way of handling different cases could cause the code to be more complex and harder for users to understand the system

Can you think of any examples where a product has overcorrected on quality?

Uber’s Self-Driving Car: Uber invested heavily in developing self-driving car technology, prioritizing safety and quality to avoid accidents. However, in their attempt to ensure safety, they overcorrected by implementing overly cautious driving algorithms. This resulted in the cars being too slow and overly conservative, which caused traffic disruptions and user dissatisfaction during testing phases.
