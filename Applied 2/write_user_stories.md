The following user stories are for a grocery story checkout system. For each of story, specify whether it is well written or poorly written. Justify your answer. You are welcome to discuss this with fellow students.

S1: As a customer, I want the system to work because that would be helpful to me.

poorly --> what does helpful mean to you?


S2: People like usable interfaces.

poorly --> not very relevant, as there is no specific user or reasoning, more of a generalised statement.


S3: As a customer, I want my payment to be processed within 10 seconds so that I can end the transaction.

well written --> specific


S4: As a checkout operator, I want the customer's change to be displayed accurately so that I can give them the right amount of money.

well written --> pretty specific


S5: As a checkout operator, I want to be notified that an item has scanned so that I know I've done my job.

mid --> "notified" is not very specific, "I've done my job" is not either. 


S6: As a store owner, I want to know the total sales for a day.

poorly --> no rationale


Based on your answers, suggest three tips for writing good user stories.

2. give a relevant reason for the want, based on the user's needs
